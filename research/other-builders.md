# Other builders: what they fit (Princess, Sunseeker, Boston Whaler, Contender and more)

Prepared 11 Nov 2026 (Brisbane time). Research only; used to add "Where you'll see it" notes and decide which product modules to add. Training in the app stays organised by **product and system**, not by builder.

**Evidence tags:** [C] confirmed on a builder/maker page or spec sheet I read. [S] snippet or listing only (US/UK dealer listings, search excerpts). [T] thin/not found. Dealer listings are often specific to one hull, region (UK/EU/US) and voltage, so none of this is "factory standard for Australia" unless stated.

## Princess (Plymouth, UK)
- **Drives/helm [C, UK price list + dealer lists]:** Volvo Penta IPS with joystick control and ACP as standard on current V/F/S-class boats (F50 spec: twin D8 IPS800, 2 x 600 mhp). Helm is **Volvo/Garmin 16 to 24 inch MFDs** with autopilot, Garmin XHD radar, Garmin AIS 800 and VHF as options/standard by model.
- **Volvo "Easy Boating" package [C, C48 price list Sep 2026]:** joystick, **Sleipner eVision variable-speed bow thruster with its own 24 V battery pack and charger**, Volvo Dynamic Positioning (DPS), Assisted Docking, Easy Connect and the **Volvo Interceptor system (Humphree) with Active Ride Control**. Standard on some, optional on others (F50 page calls it an option).
- **Stabilisation [C]:** Seakeeper 6 or Quick X13 as options; the price list notes an **11 kW generator is required** for gyro options.
- **Power [C]:** Mastervolt is Princess's exclusive power-electronics supplier (Mastervolt news item, 11th year of partnership); the contract included a "custom-built digital switching and Li-ion battery system" for the R35. AGM 140 Ah is an option in place of standard batteries on some. Onan 7 kW and 11 kW / 50 Hz generators are options (UK price list).
- **Monitoring [C]:** **Princess Connect** (telemetry, remote monitoring, maintenance alerts, digital manuals) is standard on all new Princess yachts. Supplier of the telemetry hardware not stated [T].
- **Digital switching [T]:** Mastervolt/CZone is implied by the Mastervolt agreement but no page confirms CZone, Empirbus or other on Y85/X80/S65. Treat as "identify what is fitted".
- **Spec notes:** Princess states North American boats have different specs (air conditioning, generator, electric galley) [C].

## Sunseeker (Poole, UK)
- **Drives/helm [C/S]:** Volvo Penta IPS (D11 IPS950, D13 IPS) on Predator/Manhattan with **Volvo Penta Glass Cockpit (Garmin)** 12 to 16 inch displays, Garmin GMR open-array radar and AIS 800, IPS joystick, DPS, Active Ride Control as options. Manhattan 56 offers D13-800 with **shaft or pod drive** [C: Yachting, 2026].
- **Thrusters [S]:** Side-Power bow and stern (Manhattan 55 review).
- **Stabilisation [C/S]:** Seakeeper 6 offered on Manhattan 55 / Predator 55 (optional, not standard). Trim Assist noted on the Predator 55 [C].
- **Digital switching [T]:** current standard not published. A 2012 one-off "28 Metre Yacht" used an **ABB AC500 PLC** system with Modbus and an NMEA 2000 data link [C, trade press] — an example of a non-CZone proprietary system, not a current standard. A Manhattan 55 review mentions an "M8 touchscreen" controlling lights, fans, bilges and tanks [S] without naming the system.
- **Battery security [S]:** a below-deck battery key switch is mandated on the Manhattan 55 (review).
- **Generators [S]:** Onan/Cummins generator on the Manhattan 55.

## Boston Whaler (US)
- **Engines/steering [C, MY26 spec sheets]:** Mercury Verado (V8/V10) with DTS; **Joystick Piloting for outboards** (electronic steering, Skyhook, autopilot keypad) optional. Spec sheet notes: **Simrad autopilot not compatible with Joystick Piloting; Joystick Piloting requires the Premium Package or a VesselView 4 inch display; bow thruster option not available with Joystick Piloting**.
- **Electronics [C]:** Simrad NSX/NSO evo3S (spec sheets) with Active Imaging transducer; the 2026 catalogue also mentions Raymarine — the catalogue is generic, so identify by hull. JL Audio integrated into the Simrad package.
- **Power [C]:** AGM GR31 (4 or 8) or **GR31 lithium (8)** options; **Seakeeper (SK3 on the 330 Vantage) adds four battery trays to the house bank and upgrades the charger**; "Gyro stabilizer" not compatible with the grey-water system option. Shore power with galvanic isolator and ELCI (US). 
- **Monitoring [C]:** **Whaler Watch** (Faria Beede telematics, cellular/satellite, geo-fence) and NAUTIC-ON; Mercury Theft Deterrent System. VesselView Mobile (Bluetooth) with dealer notification.
- **Safety [C]:** First Mate Safety & Security (key fob / MOB) option.

## Contender (Homestead, US)
- **Engines/steering [C/S]:** Yamaha F350/F450 with **Helm Master EX and Digital Electric Steering (DES)** on many 2026 listings; Mercury Verado with **Joystick Piloting/Skyhook** on others (35ST listing).
- **Electronics [C/S]:** Configurable Garmin or Simrad MFDs; Garmin GPSMAP 8616xsv/9219xsv with **BlueNet 30 gateway**, GSD 28 sonar, GMR Fantom/xHD3 radar, GXM 54; ICOM VHF; Fusion/JL Audio; Starlink on one 35ST.
- **Power/switching [C]:** the 2026 brochure shows "**Aegis Battery Management System and Empirbus Digital Switching**" on the 24S and other models. Livorsi 950 trim tabs, Lenco bow, Seakeeper 2 and Seakeeper Ride appear on listings [S].

## Digital switching: who uses what (from Raymarine/Garmin material)
- Raymarine LightHouse MFDs work with **YachtSense (Raymarine), CZone, EmpirBus and Böning**. **Naviop** is typically paired with Simrad/B&G. Garmin owns EmpirBus (Boat Switch product). YachtSense Digital Switching software V5.2.25 (Feb 2026): up to 12 modules stacked on one master; more than 6 needs a second bracket and 100 A total per stack [C, Raymarine software history].
